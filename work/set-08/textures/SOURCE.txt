NASA Visible Earth, "Blue Marble: Land Surface, Shallow Water, and Shaded Topography"
https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57752/land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg

NASA imagery is in the public domain and carries no licence restriction on reuse.
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Original 2048x1024 JPEG, 233 KB. Resampled in one step to 410x205 with Lanczos and
quantised to a 256-colour palette, giving a 33 KB PNG.

Sized to a budget rather than to a standard: the document it serves has to stay under
100 KB compiled, and at 410x205 it lands at 95 KB. The globe is drawn about 250 px across,
so one visible hemisphere gets roughly 250 px of screen for 180 degrees of longitude, and a
410-wide map still supplies more texels than that. Resampling is always from the original
download, never from an already-reduced copy.

Equirectangular: x maps to longitude -180..180, y maps to latitude +90..-90.
